Research

I'm a PhD candidate in Human Genetics at McGill, working in Dr. Shafaat Rabbani's lab at the Research Institute of the MUHC. Half my week is cells, blots, and mice. The other half is R, Python, and a cluster.

What I'm working on

  • uPAR in breast cancer
    What the uPAR receptor drives in invasion, stemness, and spread to bone, and what happens when you take it away.
  • Treatment resistance
    How triple-negative and HER2-positive breast tumors adapt and keep growing under therapy.
  • Drug combinations
    Pairing a genetic hit with a drug so tumors lose their escape routes, scored with synergy models.
  • Epigenetics and aging
    DNA methylation clocks and cell-free DNA markers, with collaborators across McGill.

How I work

CRISPR knockouts, western blots, qPCR, flow cytometry, confocal imaging, cloning, and mouse tumor models.

In code

RNA-seq and methylation array pipelines, single-cell workflows, survival models, and image analysis with deep learning.

Bench tools

Small calculators I kept rewriting in Excel, so here they are. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Relative expression

ΔΔCt from a Ct table, with optional primer efficiency correction. Technical replicates are averaged within each biological sample.

or paste straight from Excel (columns: Sample, Group, Gene, Ct)

GeneGroupnMean ΔCtΔΔCtFold changeSD ΔCt

Per-sample calculations
SampleGroupGeneTarget CtRef CtΔCtFold

At 100% efficiency this is standard 2−ΔΔCt. With other efficiencies, ΔCt is weighted as Ct·log2(E), which gives the Pfaffl ratio. Biological samples, not wells, are the unit for n and SD. No statistical test is run.
